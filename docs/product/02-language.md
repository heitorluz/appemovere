# Positioning and language

> Part of the Emovere product specification. Status: draft. Emovere is a self-knowledge journal that complements, and never replaces, professional care. It is not a medical device.

## Positioning

**Store listing and public materials** describe what the app does: "diário, registro, preparo da sessão" ("journal, logging, session preparation"). They do not use "terapia" (therapy), "TCC" (CBT), "ansiedade" (anxiety) or "depressão" (depression) as a promise. Category: health and wellness, not medical.

Formal consultations with regulators happen before public launch.

## Controlled language

App text is in Portuguese (pt-BR). Each phrase is followed by an English gloss.

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Ferramenta de autoconhecimento" ("Self-knowledge tool") | "Auxilia o seu tratamento" ("Supports your treatment") | Treatment claims are out of scope |
| "Registre como você está e leve para a sua sessão" ("Record how you are and take it to your session") | "Acompanha os seus sintomas" ("Tracks your symptoms") | The app does not assess or monitor symptoms |
| "Complementa, não substitui, o acompanhamento profissional" ("Complements, does not replace, professional care") | "Exercícios para a sua ansiedade" ("Exercises for your anxiety") | No treatment is suggested for a symptom or condition |
| "Pode ser um padrão; pode ser coincidência" ("It may be a pattern; it may be a coincidence") | "Você tem/apresenta…" ("You have/show…"), "isso causa…" ("this causes…") | No diagnosis or causality |
| "Você pode considerar…" ("You might consider…"), "se fizer sentido…" ("if it makes sense…") | "Você deve…" ("You must…"), "isso vai resolver…" ("this will fix it…") | Autonomy |
| "Seus registros mostram…" ("Your records show…") | "Percebi que você…" ("I noticed that you…"), "Estou preocupado com você" ("I'm worried about you") | No anthropomorphic AI ([ADR-002](../decisions/adrs.md)) |
| "Você tem registros suficientes para comparar alguns padrões" ("You have enough records to compare some patterns") | "🔥 14 dias seguidos!" ("🔥 14 days in a row!"), "meta não atingida" ("goal not met"), "dia improdutivo" ("unproductive day") | No pressure or judgment |

The same table applies to the app store listing, website, onboarding, notifications, terms of use and public materials.
