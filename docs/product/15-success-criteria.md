# Success criteria

> Part of the Emovere product specification. Status: draft. Emovere is a self-knowledge journal that complements, and never replaces, professional care. It is not a medical device.

## Prerequisites per stage

See [Phases](05-phases.md). No stage opens without its prerequisites.

## Launch checklist (every public release)

- **APA App Evaluation Model:** 5 hierarchical levels — access and background, privacy and security, clinical foundation, usability, data integration. If the app fails a level, the evaluation stops there.
- **MARS scale** (Stoyanov et al., 2015): rate the app itself and comparable apps.
- Applicable SP criteria passed and the [hazard log](../safety/hazard-log.md) reviewed.

## Learning metrics, not clinical promises

- Test participants log a check-in and generate a report without outside help.
- In usability research, people understand that the app does not replace professionals and know how to find help.
- Share of people who generate a report before a session.
- Share of insights marked "não faz sentido" ("this doesn't make sense") (insight quality).
- **Psychological safety:** share of "tem me feito pensar mais do que gostaria" ("it has made me think about it more than I'd like") answers in the rumination check, and the rate of reported unwanted effects. These are safety metrics: the goal is for them to go down, and their thresholds are in the stopping rules.
- No critical privacy or clinical safety incident without containment, communication and formal analysis.

The following are not success criteria: screen time, streaks, dependence on the product, symptom reduction, or any clinical metric without an appropriate study, ethics review and independent validation.
