# Appendix A — Definition of done for sensitive features

> Part of the Emovere product specification. Status: draft. Emovere is a self-knowledge journal that complements, and never replaces, professional care. It is not a medical device.

A feature that processes the journal, calculates or drafts an insight, suggests an action or shows crisis resources only enters a stage that involves other people when it has:

- its goal, benefit and foreseeable harm described;
- the related hazards updated in the [hazard log](../safety/hazard-log.md);
- the clinical and product decision recorded;
- minimal data, purpose and retention approved;
- normal, edge and adversarial test cases;
- failure handling and a fallback **without AI**;
- the applicable psychological safety criteria (SP) passed;
- interface text checked against [Language](02-language.md) and reviewed for accessibility, UX and clinical content;
- no number shown outside the corresponding `Pattern`;
- a success metric that does not depend on increasing compulsive use;
- an identified owner for operations, incidents and rollback.
