# Privacy and LGPD

> Part of the Emovere product specification. Status: draft. Emovere is a self-knowledge journal that complements, and never replaces, professional care. It is not a medical device.

## Background

- **Fact:** health data is sensitive personal data. Consent as a legal basis must be specific and prominent (LGPD, art. 11, I).
- **Fact:** under ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 2/2022, high-risk processing (at least one general criterion plus one specific criterion, which include sensitive data and emerging technology) loses the simplified regime, and the obligation to appoint a data protection officer applies again.
- **Hypothesis:** the app counts as high-risk processing. This means a data protection officer (DPO), a data protection impact report and legal review before the closed beta.
- **Hypothesis:** with data only on the device, the project has no access to the content, which greatly reduces the risk of a leak. The project's legal role and the obligations that still apply are confirmed in legal review.
- **Fact:** Apple forbids storing personal health information in iCloud and using health data for advertising or data mining (App Review Guideline 5.1.3).
- International data transfer (art. 33) happens only in Phase 2, if the AI provider is outside Brazil. It is minimized because the insight is drafted from the `Pattern` only.

## What leaves the phone in Phase 1

The privacy policy lists exactly this:

- Email sent to support (by the person).
- Reports of unwanted effects and answers to the rumination check — only with consent, anonymous and aggregate.
- Help access counter — only with consent.
- Crash reports, if any — only with consent and without content.
- The read of the remote configuration file and the age signal from the app stores.
- The IP address in these connections.

## Commitments

- A data map and a purpose for each field; collect only what is needed.
- Granular consent, with simple revocation and its effect recorded.
- Portable export (an encrypted file generated by the person) and verifiable deletion (erasing the key and the local database), including derived data.
- Encryption, separation of environments, minimal internal access, vendor review and data processing agreements.
- A data protection impact report (RIPD) before the closed beta, updated in Phase 2.
- Aggregate analytics only; no sale of data, no ads, no behavioral profiling.
- The money flow is kept separate from the data flow: the app never combines socioeconomic data with health data.
